What does this have to do with ChatGPT?

It is the same idea: predicting the next word. The difference is scale. This machine looks 1 to 3 words back and has read — words. A large language model looks hundreds of thousands of words back and has read trillions. That is why it writes so well, and that is also why it makes things up with the same confidence: it does not look up facts, it chooses the most probable continuation.

  • Temperature 0: always the most probable word. Repetitive, predictable.
  • High temperature: it risks rare words. More "creative," less sense.
  • Looking 3 words back: more coherent sentences, but it starts copying the original text. This is the memorization problem.

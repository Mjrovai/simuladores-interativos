Q, K, V

Every token is projected three ways: a query (“what am I looking for?”), a key (“what do I offer?”) and a value (“what do I pass on?”). The score is q·k / √d, softmaxed over the past.

Why the triangle

The mask sets the future to −∞ before the softmax, so a token can only attend to itself and what came before. That is what makes generation possible: the same weights work for every prefix.

Layer 1 vs layer 2

Head patterns change with depth. Early heads usually track nearby words and punctuation; the second layer mixes those signals into something closer to “what kind of word comes next”.

What it cannot do

198k parameters, 72k tokens of text, a 32-token memory. It has no world knowledge — it learned the rhythm of one author's sentences and little else. That is the point: you can see all of it.

Flying the 14-bis

She is marginal — 50 hp, heavy, and only just flies. Build speed on the grass, rotate gently, and stay low. Full throttle is almost always needed.

  • W/↑ Pull — nose up
  • S/↓ Push — nose down
  • A/← Roll left
  • D/→ Roll right
  • Q/E Rudder left / right
  • Shift/Ctrl Throttle up / down
  • G Engine on / off
  • Space Brake (on ground)
  • C Camera
  • R Reset

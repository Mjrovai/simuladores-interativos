Counting firsts is one way to read the race, not the only one. The Soviet programme collected most of the early records; the American programme spent those same years building the industrial depth that put two people on the Moon in 1969. Both sides also lost crews — Komarov, the Apollo 1 fire and Soyuz 11 are in the data, because a scoreboard that hides them is a worse lesson.
